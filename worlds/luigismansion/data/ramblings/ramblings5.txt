Have you ever thought about what ghosts are made of Luigi? It's fascinating stuff. Ectoplasm composes the entirety of every ghost, from their eyes to their projectiles. Even their clothes are made from the stuff! It's a very strange kind of matter, completely different from what you and I are made from. That chill you feel when a ghost is near you? That's ectoplasm drawing the heat away from you. Unlike the molecules we are made of, which absorb energy and become more volatile, ectoplasm stabilizes and solidifies the more heat it receives. That's why ghosts chase us. They're trying to solidfy into a much more real form to wreak havoc on us! I actually found a way to use ectoplasm to create too. Capture enough of the stuff, use it like paint, apply heat and voila, you've got a moving creation all your own! I made a paintbrush with it but gave it away. That's a different story though.
