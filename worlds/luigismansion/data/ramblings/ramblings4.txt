Hey, Luigi! I just got back from a a shopping trip and I met the cutest little youngster! I was walking through the mall when I spotted the little tyke looking around for his papa. At first I thought it was Mario but as I got closer, I realized he was green! Some kind of large turtle boy. When I asked if he was okay, he said he lost his papa to a big, mean man in red and couldn't find him anywhere! I felt bad for the little guy, so I gave him the only thing I had on me - my magic paintbrush. Haven't used it since I accidentally painted these ghosts into existed, but such an eager little boy couldn't possibly come up with anything terrible to draw. I'm sure he'll have a wonderful time drawing little stick figures. He said he was off to Isle Delfino for a vacation as soon as he finds his father. What a wonderful son!
