# Super Mario Galaxy 2

## Where is the settings page?

The [player settings page for this game](../player-settings) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

By default, all Power Stars, Grand Stars, Checkpoints, Comet Medals and Hungry Lumas are locations that could contain
an item. Optionally, players may also add Green Star locations, Mailtoad Letters and chatting with Starship Mario 
passengers as locations. Power Stars and Grand stars are always added at items, as well as 1-up mushrooms and bundles of 
coins and starbits as filler items. There are a number of extra options to lock Green Star locations, Hungry Lumas, 
as well as Mario and Yoshi's abilities, such as Fire Flowers, Dash Peppers, or Mario's Sideflip.

There is also an option to shuffle galaxies amongst worlds

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Power Stars, Grand Stars, Green Stars, Mario's movement abilities and power-ups and a variety of other things can be 
found in other players' worlds.

## What does another world's item look like in Super Mario Galaxy 2
<!-- This might be changed depending on what we decide to do later -->
Items belonging to other worlds will not look any different from how the location containing the item would look in vanilla.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
When receiving a Power Star, Green Star or Grand star, the Power Star counter in the top left of the screen will 
increase. In the bottom right corner, a small notification will appear to notify you of what you have sent, or received
